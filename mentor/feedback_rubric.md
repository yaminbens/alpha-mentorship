# Feedback rubric
Score each dimension 0 (missing), 1 (partial), or 2 (clear), with one specific comment.
| Dimension | Clear work includes |
|---|---|
| Question and prediction | A testable comparison and a prediction recorded first |
| Controls | Fixed count/box/model and documented preparation and intervention |
| Reproducibility | Settings, units, observation window and repeat described |
| Evidence | Traceable table, readable plot and screenshots or saved observations |
| Interpretation | Claims follow observations; ambiguity and fluctuations acknowledged |
| Reflection | A limitation and a specific useful next measurement |

A result contrary to the prediction can receive full credit. The total is a teaching
indicator, not an official Alpha grading scheme. Give one priority revision at a time.
