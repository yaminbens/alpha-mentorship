# Narrowing the project
Begin with the question students can measure: how does a specified neighbour-persistence
measure vary across energy preparations at fixed density? Define k neighbours, a lag,
boundaries, temperature convention and independent repeats before implementation.
A first result is a reproducible plot with its conditions and uncertainty, not a
melting temperature. Ask what comparison could falsify the prediction.

If students struggle, retain two conditions and describe measurement limitations;
record the agreed scope change. If they progress, repeat preparations and compare
observation windows before adding an estimator or more complex model. Keep ML and
MI as optional extensions tied to a question. Do not equate classification accuracy
with accurate entropy or free energy. Project choice is provisional until discussed
with students and matched to official program expectations.
