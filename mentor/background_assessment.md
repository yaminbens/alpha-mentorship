# Getting to know the students
Ask conversationally; this is not an entrance test.
1. What interested you in the reading? What remained confusing?
2. Have you seen force, energy and temperature in class? Explain one in your words.
3. In a graph of temperature versus time, what do the axes tell you?
4. Have you used Python, spreadsheets or notebooks? What did you make?
5. Is reading scientific English comfortable? Would Hebrew prompts help?
6. What would you like to discover about materials or simulations?

If graphs are new, work through the first notebook's labelled axes together. If coding
is new, focus on executing and changing one value. Strong programmers may explain
reproducibility or help debug, but should still record predictions before execution.
Keep individual responses in private notes, not in this repository.
