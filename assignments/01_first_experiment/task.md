# First experiment at home
**Question:** how do arrangement and motion change when energy is added at fixed
particle number and box size? Allow about 60–90 minutes across two tasks.

## Task 1 — explore and record
Open Schroeder's simulator from the course site. Use the supplied initial state
or the documented preset. Write a prediction before adding energy. Record N, L,
model settings and a screenshot. Observe movement and whether neighbours change.
Write what you saw and one uncertainty in your saved Task 1 notebook.

## Task 2 — compare
Keep N, L, gravity, bonds, boundary rules and observation procedure fixed. Use three
energy preparations. Record every intervention; temperature is measured, not assumed
constant. After each intervention record a settling interval and an equal observation
window. Record measured T and potential energy per particle. Repeat one preparation
from the same starting state. Retain failures or ambiguous observations with notes.

Plot potential energy per particle against measured temperature. Add screenshots,
settings and raw data. Explain the comparison in about 150 words and propose a
structural or dynamical measurement. An energy plot is not itself structural order.
A short trajectory does not determine an equilibrium melting temperature.

Save notebook text and outputs, separately save data/images, reopen your notebook,
share with the designated mentor account and send its link through the agreed
private channel. Use the downloadable report/results templates if Colab access fails.
