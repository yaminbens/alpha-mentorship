# First experiment at home
**Question:** what changes when energy is added at fixed particle count and box size?
Work in order. You may answer in English or Hebrew; short sentences are welcome.

1. **Task 1: watch and describe (20–30 minutes).** Compare 0 and 24 Faster clicks,
   loading the same starting file before each run. Write a prediction, record settings,
   save two screenshots, and describe motion separately from arrangement.
2. **Task 2: measure and compare (60–90 minutes; split into two sessions if useful).**
   Start fresh for each of four runs: 0, 8, 24, then 8 Faster clicks. Each run has
   1000 preparation steps, 1000 settling steps and 40 readings separated by 25 steps.
   Use the notebook to calculate mean temperature and mean potential energy per particle.
   Fill four result rows, plot them, and write about 150 words explaining the evidence.

The full numbered procedures, notebook launch buttons and help instructions are on
[Task 1](https://yaminbens.github.io/alpha-mentorship/task-1.html) and
[Task 2](https://yaminbens.github.io/alpha-mentorship/task-2.html).
Use [the exploration worksheet](exploration_template.md) and
[the report template](report_template.md); the same prompts are in the notebooks.

Keep N=64, box side=12, gravity=0, time step=0.005 and the model settings unchanged.
Record deviations or failures. Temperature is measured, not set by the Faster button.
The same-state repeat checks the procedure, not independent-run uncertainty.
An energy plot is not a direct measure of structural order; short runs do not establish
an equilibrium melting temperature.

Save your own notebook copy and supporting data/images. Reopen it, share with the
mentor account given in class, and send the link through the private channel.
If stuck, submit the partial work with the step number and error or difficulty.
