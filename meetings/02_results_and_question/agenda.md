# Follow-up — results and question
Allow about 45 minutes: 10 for student findings, 10 for comparing controls and repeats,
15 for selecting a measurement and 10 for agreeing the proposal outline. Ask students
to show one surprising observation and its settings. Choose a neighbour definition,
a lag and fixed density before discussing a temperature trend. Record what remains
unresolved. The result is a testable question, not a claim that a transition was found.
