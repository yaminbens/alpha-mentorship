# Provisional research plan
Question: at fixed density, how does neighbour persistence change across energy
preparations in a 2D model? Student discussion may refine this before execution.
Hypothesis: higher measured temperature may accompany more neighbour changes over a
fixed lag. This is a prediction, not an established result.

Define each particle's k=4 nearest neighbours at the initial observation time. At a
specified lag, count retained neighbours, divide by k and average over particles.
Keep N, box, boundary rules, k, lag and interaction model fixed. Record ties and the
distance rule. Use measured temperature with its window. Prepare independent runs
and compare within-run fluctuations with between-run variation; determine adequate
sampling through diagnostics, not a promised universal sample count.

Expected figure: persistence versus measured temperature, with preparation and
uncertainty described. Compare lag dependence before interpreting a sharp change.
The initial small exercise demonstrates analysis, not an equilibrium phase boundary.
No new production campaign is launched by this plan.
