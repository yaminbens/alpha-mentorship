# Timeline
| Stage | Target | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Introductory meeting | Before December 6–7 seminar; exact date to coordinate | Saved notebook and homework understood |
| Home experiment | About one week after meeting | Task 1/2 notebooks and data |
| Follow-up | Before December 6 | Reviewed observations and a narrowed question |

| Summer research | Official dates not supplied | Agreed study, report or poster |

If the introductory date leaves less than a week, agree a smaller first deliverable
explicitly rather than silently overloading students. Dates and workload estimates
are planning aids, not scientific convergence criteria.
