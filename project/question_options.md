# Feasible questions
| Question | Observable and control | Minimum output | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| How does added energy change motion? | Measured T and U/N at fixed N,L | Table and plot | Energy is not structural order |
| Do neighbours persist? | Fraction of k nearest neighbours retained at fixed lag | Persistence versus measured T | Choice of k, lag and history matters |
| Does observation duration affect our conclusion? | Same measure in several windows | Window comparison | Adjacent samples are correlated |

Choose one with students. The provisional core is neighbour persistence. Additional
classifiers or MI estimates should answer a new defined question, not replace controls.
