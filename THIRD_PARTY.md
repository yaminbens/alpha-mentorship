# Third-party sources
vendor/lennard_jones_fluid_lab/ contains unmodified files from Gianmarc Grazioli's
LennardJonesFluidLab, commit 9f3f932bb3ffdf47bc0de23378aba196bdc9452b. The upstream
MIT license is retained beside them. Source: https://github.com/ggrazioli/LennardJonesFluidLab.
Our notebooks adapt the teaching workflow and call that pinned code; they do not
copy the full original lab assignment. Pressure output is not used in conclusions.

Schroeder's simulation and the linked papers/articles remain external resources.
Browser screenshots/recordings used here are attributed educational excerpts of
that simulation, not relicensed copies of its source. Original course figures are
labelled as illustrations or generated observations. No complete article is copied.
Fonts in PDF exports use DejaVu Sans from the build system.
