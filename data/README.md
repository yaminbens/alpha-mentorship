# Data and provenance
examples/ contains small labelled teaching datasets. metadata.json records their
source, model, seed, settings and measurement window. Synthetic examples are not
physical measurements. Browser rehearsal outputs are identified separately.
Student data belong in their saved notebooks or private Drive files, not this public
repository. Keep original exports alongside transformed tables. A notebook link alone
does not preserve runtime files.

results_template.csv fields: condition and repeat identify preparation; N is total
particle count; L is side length in sigma; T_reduced is k_B T/epsilon; U_per_particle
is potential energy/(N epsilon); t_start and t_end are reduced simulation times.
preparation records interventions; observations records qualitative findings.
