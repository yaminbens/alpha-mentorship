## Your question
What changes when we add energy to an ordered collection of atoms?

1. Open [Schroeder's simulator](https://physics.weber.edu/schroeder/md/) and follow
   [our browser guide](browser.html). Load the supplied starting state.
2. Record particle number, box size and starting-state filename in your notebook.
3. **Predict first:** will the particles move faster? Will their arrangement change?
4. Start the simulation. Observe the starting condition, then add energy as described
   in the guide while holding count and box fixed.
5. Write what changed in motion and in arrangement. Include a screenshot and its
   settings. Keep a separate saved copy of any image/data file.
6. Describe one uncertainty and propose one quantity to measure in Task 2.

## Save this evidence
A prediction, settings, one before/after comparison and one open question. Do not
call a fast-moving configuration liquid solely because it moves quickly.
