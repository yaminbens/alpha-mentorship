**At home · about 40–60 minutes · use your saved Task 1 preparation**

## Compare fairly
Keep particle number, box size, gravity, bonds and model fixed. Compare three energy
preparations using the [browser guide](browser.html). Record the interventions and
use the same settling and observation intervals. Measure temperature; the energy
controls do not impose a constant temperature.

## Record your evidence
For each condition record measured temperature, potential energy per particle,
observation window and arrangement/motion. Repeat one condition from the same
starting preparation. Save screenshots and raw data. Keep unexpected results.

The notebook contains an empty table: enter your own values before making the plot.
Plot potential energy per particle against measured temperature with labelled units.
An energy plot is not itself a measurement of structural order.

## Explain in about 150 words
What happened? Did it match your prediction? Which observation supports your claim?
What remains uncertain? What would you measure next? Short finite-system observations
do not determine an equilibrium melting temperature.
